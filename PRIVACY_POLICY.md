# Privacy Policy for Quota Tracker

**Last Updated:** July 28, 2026

Quota Tracker ("we", "us", or "our"), available at domain [seyone.dev](https://seyone.dev), is committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you use the Quota Tracker mobile application and Wear OS companion app (collectively, the "Application").

## 1. Summary

Quota Tracker is designed with a **privacy-first, offline-local architecture**. The Application does not collect, store, transmit, track, or share any of your personal data, usage analytics, or device identifiers with external servers or third parties.

## 2. Information Collection and Use

- **Local Storage:** All application data—including your quota targets, time logs, settings, and preferences—is stored strictly on your local device using Android's local Room database and DataStore.
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information to use the Application.
- **No Telemetry or Tracking:** The Application does not use analytics SDKs, advertising IDs, tracking cookies, or third-party diagnostic trackers.

## 3. Data Transfer & Device Synchronization

- **Wear OS Synchronization:** If you use the Wear OS companion app, quota state and time log actions are synchronized locally between your paired phone and smartwatch using Google Play Services Wearable Data Layer API. This communication occurs strictly between your own paired devices.
- **Data Export & Backup:** The Application allows you to manually export your data into JSON backups or CSV reports. These files are saved locally on your device storage and are only accessible by you.

## 4. Permissions

The Application may request the following standard Android permissions solely to perform core features:

- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Used strictly to send optional local notifications (e.g., Saturday check-in reminder). No notification data leaves your device.
- **Wearable Communication:** Used to send and receive local log updates with your paired Wear OS device.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The Application does not integrate with any third-party advertising networks, analytics providers, or external data brokers.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The Application does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published by updating this document in our official repository and website.
